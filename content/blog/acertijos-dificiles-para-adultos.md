## Hard Riddles for Adults with Answers

Challenge yourself with these hard riddles that are perfect for adults. Each one requires careful thinking and a fresh perspective.

## 1. What is always in front of you but cannot be seen?

**Answer:** The future

## 2. What is it that you can break without even touching it?

**Answer:** A promise

## 3. What has a neck but no head, two arms but no hands?

**Answer:** A shirt

## 4. What is seen once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M

## 5. What is full of keys but cannot open a single door?

**Answer:** A piano

## 6. What can you make that you can never see?

**Answer:** A noise

## 7. What is heavy forward but backward is not?

**Answer:** Ton

## 8. What comes down but never goes up?

**Answer:** Rain

## 9. What is it that when you take away the whole, you still have some left over?

**Answer:** Some

## 10. What is black when it is clean and white when it is dirty?

**Answer:** A chalkboard

## 11. What is it that given one, you will have either two or none?

**Answer:** A choice

## 12. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A guitar
