## 1. What is full of holes but still holds water?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 2. What can fill a room but takes up no space?

**Answer:** Light

## 3. What has a ring but no finger?

**Answer:** A telephone

## 4. What has a face like a clock, hands like a clock, but tells time differently?

**Answer:** A calendar

## 5. What gets wetter the more it dries?

**Answer:** A towel

## 6. What begins with T, ends with T, and is full of T?

**Answer:** A teapot

## 7. What can you keep after giving to someone?

**Answer:** Your word

## 8. What is seen in the middle of March and April but not at the beginning or end of either month?

**Answer:** The letter R

## 9. What is easy to get into but hard to get out of?

**Answer:** Trouble

## 10. What has many teeth but cannot bite?

**Answer:** A zipper

## 11. What can travel around the world while staying in a corner?

**Answer:** A stamp

## 12. What has ears but cannot hear?

**Answer:** Corn
