## 🍕 Baking Competition (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What can you slice but never touch?

**Answer:** Time

## 2. What can you boil but not burn?

**Answer:** Water

## 3. What has a bunch of spots but is not a dice?

**Answer:** A strawberry

## 4. What has a face but no eyes in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A clock

## 5. What has a head but no body in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A head of lettuce

## 6. What has a rind but no ring?

**Answer:** A watermelon

## 7. What has a bunch of eyes but cannot see?

**Answer:** A potato

## 8. What can you blend but never mix?

**Answer:** A smoothie

## 9. What has a heart but no blood on the plate?

**Answer:** An artichoke

## 10. What is full of holes but still holds water?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 11. What is full of holes but holds water in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A colander

## 12. What has a core but is not an apple?

**Answer:** An apple (it is!)
