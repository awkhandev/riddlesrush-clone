## 🧸 Bedtime (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What can you catch but not throw?

**Answer:** A cold

## 2. What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M

## 3. What is full of holes but still holds water?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 4. What can fill a room but takes up no space?

**Answer:** Light

## 5. What has a ring but no finger?

**Answer:** A telephone

## 6. What has a face like a clock, hands like a clock, but tells time differently?

**Answer:** A calendar

## 7. What gets wetter the more it dries?

**Answer:** A towel

## 8. What begins with T, ends with T, and is full of T?

**Answer:** A teapot

## 9. What can you keep after giving to someone?

**Answer:** Your word

## 10. What is seen in the middle of March and April but not at the beginning or end of either month?

**Answer:** The letter R

## 11. What is easy to get into but hard to get out of?

**Answer:** Trouble

## 12. What has many teeth but cannot bite?

**Answer:** A zipper
