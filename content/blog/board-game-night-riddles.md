## 🧩 Board Game Night (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has a head and a tail that every family has?

**Answer:** A coin

## 2. What has a spine but no bones on the bookshelf?

**Answer:** A book

## 3. What can you taste but never smell at dinner?

**Answer:** A word

## 4. What gets smaller every time it takes a bath in the bathroom?

**Answer:** A bar of soap

## 5. What can you hear but not touch or see at home?

**Answer:** Your voice

## 6. What is always in front of you but can't be seen by the family?

**Answer:** The future

## 7. What is full of holes but still holds water in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 8. What can fill a room but takes up no space at home?

**Answer:** Light

## 9. What has hands but can't clap at the dinner table?

**Answer:** A clock

## 10. What gets wetter the more it dries in the laundry?

**Answer:** A towel

## 11. What has a neck but no head in the pantry?

**Answer:** A bottle

## 12. What can you keep after giving to your child?

**Answer:** Your word
