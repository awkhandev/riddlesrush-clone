Ready for a real challenge? These 🧠 riddles are built for grown-up brains — the kind that enjoy a puzzle with some teeth. Whether you're looking to stump your friends or just want a mental workout, this collection delivers.


## 🧠 20 (With Answers)

Test your wits with these — but don't feel bad if you need to check the answers. That's what they're there for.

## 1. What is always coming but never arrives?

**Answer:** Tomorrow

## 2. What is always running but never moves?

**Answer:** Water

## 3. What comes once in a year, twice in a week, but never in a day?

**Answer:** The letter E

## 4. What has a head and a tail but no body?

**Answer:** A coin

## 5. What is the end of everything?

**Answer:** The letter G

## 6. What can you never finish?

**Answer:** An autobiography

## 7. What can you hold without using your hands?

**Answer:** Your breath

## 8. What has a heart that doesn't beat?

**Answer:** An artichoke

## 9. What is easy to lift but hard to throw?

**Answer:** A feather

## 10. What has many keys but can't open a single lock?

**Answer:** A computer keyboard

## 11. What starts with an 'e', ends with an 'e', and contains one letter?

**Answer:** An envelope

## 12. What has many rings but no fingers?

**Answer:** A tree trunk

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Stuck on a riddle? Here are some strategies that might help:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
