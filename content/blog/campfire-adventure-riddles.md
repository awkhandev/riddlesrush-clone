## 🔥 Campfire Adventure (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has many arms but no hands in the garden?

**Answer:** A tree

## 2. What has a neck but no head in the forest?

**Answer:** A bottle tree

## 3. What falls in a forest but never gets hurt?

**Answer:** A leaf

## 4. What can travel around the world while staying in a flower?

**Answer:** A seed

## 5. What has a tongue but cannot talk in the forest?

**Answer:** A leaf

## 6. What has a bark but no bite?

**Answer:** A tree

## 7. What can you hear but not touch in the woods?

**Answer:** The wind

## 8. What is always running but never moves in nature?

**Answer:** A river

## 9. What comes down but never goes up in the sky?

**Answer:** Rain

## 10. What has roots but no legs?

**Answer:** A tree

## 11. What is full of holes but still holds water in the garden?

**Answer:** Soil

## 12. What has a head and a tail but no body in the pond?

**Answer:** A tadpole
