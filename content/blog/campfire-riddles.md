## 🔥 Campfire Riddles

Perfect riddles for outdoor adventures and cozy campfire gatherings.

## 1. The Warm Glow

I crackle and pop on a cool camping night. I provide warmth and light, a beautiful sight. What am I?

**Answer:** Campfire

## 2. The Marshmallow Treat

I'm white and fluffy, and I'm best when toasted brown. Put me between chocolate and graham crackers — best treat in town. What am I?

**Answer:** S'more

## 3. The Night Sky

I'm full of stars on a clear camping night. I'm vast and dark, a beautiful sight. What am I?

**Answer:** The Night Sky

## 4. The Portable Shelter

I keep you dry when it rains outdoors. I'm set up with poles and pegs, and I come in many colors and sizes. What am I?

**Answer:** Tent

## 5. The Sleeping Bag

I keep you warm when you sleep on the ground. I'm soft and cozy, and I zip all around. What am I?

**Answer:** Sleeping Bag

## 6. The Forest Friend

I have a bushy tail and I love acorns. I climb trees and chatter in the morning. What am I?

**Answer:** Squirrel

## 7. The Crackling Sound

I pop and crackle in the fire pit. I was once a living tree, now I keep campers warm and lit. What am I?

**Answer:** Firewood

## 8. The Morning Brew

Campers drink me hot in the morning. I'm brewed from beans and give energy without warning. What am I?

**Answer:** Coffee
