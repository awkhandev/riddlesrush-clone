## 🍕 Carnival Food (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has a pit but is not a hole in the ground?

**Answer:** A peach

## 2. What can you grate but never scratch?

**Answer:** Cheese

## 3. What can you fry but never burn?

**Answer:** An egg (sunny side up)

## 4. What has ears but cannot hear in the garden?

**Answer:** Corn

## 5. What has a tongue but cannot talk at the table?

**Answer:** A shoe (slipper)

## 6. What can you spread but not throw on bread?

**Answer:** Butter

## 7. What has a peel but no pain?

**Answer:** A banana

## 8. What has a bunch of folds but is not a shirt?

**Answer:** A dumpling

## 9. What gets wetter the more it dries in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A dish towel

## 10. What can fill a room but takes up no space at dinner?

**Answer:** The aroma of food

## 11. What has a shell but no animal inside?

**Answer:** A coconut

## 12. What can you whisk but never see?

**Answer:** An egg
