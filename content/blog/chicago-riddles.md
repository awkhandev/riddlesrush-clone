The classic "What Am I?" format — a series of clues describing something without naming it. This 🌬️ collection is packed with brain teasers that'll have you guessing, debating, and second-guessing yourself. How many can you solve?


## 🌬️ Chicago Riddles

Chicago riddles about deep dish, lakefront breezes, and skyline giants.

## 1. The Windy City

They call me the Windy City, though my breeze isn't always the reason. I'm known for deep-dish pizza and a skyline that's pleasing. What city am I?

**Answer:** Chicago

## 2. The Big Bean

I'm a giant bean-shaped mirror in the middle of a park. Tourists love to take selfies, and my surface makes a spark. What am I?

**Answer:** Cloud Gate (The Bean)

## 3. The Tall Tower

I was once the tallest building in the world, standing proud and bright. My antenna reaches the clouds on a Chicago night. What am I?

**Answer:** Willis Tower (Sears Tower)

## 4. The Deep Dish

I'm a pizza style born in Chicago, with a thick crust and lots of cheese. Layers of tomato sauce on top, if you please. What am I?

**Answer:** Deep Dish Pizza

## 5. The Lake Effect

I'm one of the Great Lakes, bordering Chicago's eastern shore. My waters are blue and vast, and I provide so much more. What lake am I?

**Answer:** Lake Michigan

## 6. The Green View

I'm a famous road trip starting in Chicago, heading west to Santa Monica. I'm historic and iconic, a route that's truly panoramic. What am I?

**Answer:** Route 66
