## 🎄 Christmas Morning (With Answers)

Ready to put your brain to work? See how many of these you can solve without help.

## 1. What falls in winter but never gets hurt?

**Answer:** Snow

## 2. What is a parent's favorite Christmas carol?

**Answer:** Silent Night

## 3. What do snowmen like to do on the weekend?

**Answer:** Chill out

## 4. What do you call a snowman with a six-pack?

**Answer:** An abdominal snowman

## 5. Why did the turkey join the band?

**Answer:** Because it had the drumsticks

## 6. What do elves learn in school?

**Answer:** The elf-abet

## 7. What do you get when you cross a vampire with a snowman?

**Answer:** Frostbite

## 8. What is a Christmas tree's favorite candy?

**Answer:** O-ornaments

## 9. Why was the Christmas tree so bad at knitting?

**Answer:** Because it always dropped its needles

## 10. What do you call a dog that works at a mall?

**Answer:** A mall-y

## 11. What do ghosts serve for dessert?

**Answer:** Boo-berry pie

## 12. What do you call a reindeer with no eyes?

**Answer:** No eye-deer

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Having trouble? Try these tips before giving up:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
