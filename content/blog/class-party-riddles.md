Kids love a good riddle, and this collection is loaded with them. These 🧸 brain teasers are designed to get young minds thinking — tricky enough to be fun, but never so hard they give up. Grab the kids and start guessing!


## 🧸 Class Party (With Answers)

Think laterally, question your assumptions, and don't trust your first instinct. That's the secret to these.

## 1. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A bottle

## 2. What has a face and two hands but no arms or legs?

**Answer:** A clock

## 3. What gets smaller every time it takes a bath?

**Answer:** A bar of soap

## 4. What gets sharper the more you use it?

**Answer:** Your brain

## 5. What can be cracked, made, told, and played?

**Answer:** A joke

## 6. What can you catch but not throw?

**Answer:** A cold

## 7. What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M

## 8. What is full of holes but still holds water?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 9. What can fill a room but takes up no space?

**Answer:** Light

## 10. What has a ring but no finger?

**Answer:** A telephone

## 11. What has a face like a clock, hands like a clock, but tells time differently?

**Answer:** A calendar

## 12. What gets wetter the more it dries?

**Answer:** A towel

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Having trouble? Try these tips before giving up:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
