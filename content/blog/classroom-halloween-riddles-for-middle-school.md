## 🎃 Classroom Halloween (With Answers)

Here are some brain teasers that'll keep you guessing. Try to solve each one before peeking at the answer!

## 1. What do you get when you cross a Christmas tree with an iPad?

**Answer:** A PinePad

## 2. What do you get when you cross a snowman and a vampire?

**Answer:** Frostbite

## 3. What is a Christmas tree's favorite candy?

**Answer:** O-ornaments

## 4. What do you get if you eat Christmas decorations?

**Answer:** Tinsel-itis

## 5. What do you call an obnoxious reindeer?

**Answer:** Rude-olph

## 6. What do you call a snowman with a six-pack?

**Answer:** An abdominal snowman

## 7. What do elves learn in school?

**Answer:** The elf-abet

## 8. What do you get when you cross a vampire with a snowman?

**Answer:** Frostbite

## 9. Why was the Christmas tree so bad at knitting?

**Answer:** Because it always dropped its needles

## 10. What do you call a dog that works at a mall?

**Answer:** A mall-y

## 11. What do ghosts serve for dessert?

**Answer:** Boo-berry pie

## 12. Why did the turkey join the band?

**Answer:** Because it had the drumsticks

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Stuck on a riddle? Here are some strategies that might help:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
