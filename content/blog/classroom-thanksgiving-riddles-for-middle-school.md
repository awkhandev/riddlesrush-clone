## 🦃 Classroom Thanksgiving (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What do you call a reindeer with no eyes?

**Answer:** No eye-deer

## 2. What is Frosty the Snowman's favorite breakfast?

**Answer:** Ice crispies

## 3. What did the gingerbread man put on his bed?

**Answer:** A cookie sheet

## 4. What kind of music do elves listen to?

**Answer:** Wrap music

## 5. What do elves learn in school?

**Answer:** The elf-abet

## 6. What do you call a snowman with a six-pack?

**Answer:** An abdominal snowman

## 7. What do you get when you cross a vampire with a snowman?

**Answer:** Frostbite

## 8. What is a Christmas tree's favorite candy?

**Answer:** O-ornaments

## 9. Why was the Christmas tree so bad at knitting?

**Answer:** Because it always dropped its needles

## 10. What do you call a dog that works at a mall?

**Answer:** A mall-y

## 11. What do ghosts serve for dessert?

**Answer:** Boo-berry pie

## 12. Why did the turkey join the band?

**Answer:** Because it had the drumsticks
