## 🧸 Community Helper (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has a ring but no finger?

**Answer:** A telephone

## 2. What has a face like a clock, hands like a clock, but tells time differently?

**Answer:** A calendar

## 3. What gets wetter the more it dries?

**Answer:** A towel

## 4. What begins with T, ends with T, and is full of T?

**Answer:** A teapot

## 5. What can you keep after giving to someone?

**Answer:** Your word

## 6. What is seen in the middle of March and April but not at the beginning or end of either month?

**Answer:** The letter R

## 7. What is easy to get into but hard to get out of?

**Answer:** Trouble

## 8. What has many teeth but cannot bite?

**Answer:** A zipper

## 9. What can travel around the world while staying in a corner?

**Answer:** A stamp

## 10. What has ears but cannot hear?

**Answer:** Corn

## 11. What has a spine but no bones?

**Answer:** A book

## 12. What can you hear but not touch or see?

**Answer:** Your voice
