## ⭐ Dallas Riddles

Riddles covering big skies, skyline icons, and Texas pride.

## 1. The Big D

I'm known as the "Big D" in the heart of Texas. I'm famous for cowboys, barbecue, and a skyline that's tremendous. What city am I?

**Answer:** Dallas

## 2. The Grassy Knoll

I'm a park in Dallas with a famous history. A president's life ended here, and the world watched in misery. What am I?

**Answer:** Dealey Plaza

## 3. The Reunion Tower

I'm a ball-shaped tower in the Dallas skyline. My lights shine bright at night, and I'm a Texas landmark fine. What am I?

**Answer:** Reunion Tower

## 4. The Blue Star

I'm a team in Dallas that wears blue and silver with pride. I play football on Sundays and have a star as my guide. What team am I?

**Answer:** Dallas Cowboys

## 5. The State Fair

I'm a fair held every fall in Dallas, with a giant Ferris wheel and fried food stalls. What fair am I?

**Answer:** State Fair of Texas
