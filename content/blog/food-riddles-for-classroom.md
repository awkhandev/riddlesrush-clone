## 🍕 Food (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What can you spread but not throw on bread?

**Answer:** Butter

## 2. What has a peel but no pain?

**Answer:** A banana

## 3. What has a bunch of folds but is not a shirt?

**Answer:** A dumpling

## 4. What gets wetter the more it dries in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A dish towel

## 5. What can fill a room but takes up no space at dinner?

**Answer:** The aroma of food

## 6. What has a shell but no animal inside?

**Answer:** A coconut

## 7. What can you whisk but never see?

**Answer:** An egg

## 8. What can you mash but not crush?

**Answer:** Potatoes

## 9. What has a head and a tail but no body on the plate?

**Answer:** A coin (left as a tip)

## 10. What has many keys but can't open a single lock in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A piano (cookie sheet)

## 11. What can you crack without touching?

**Answer:** A walnut

## 12. What has a bunch of layers but is not a cake?

**Answer:** An onion
