## 🍕 Food (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What can you blend but never mix?

**Answer:** A smoothie

## 2. What has a heart but no blood on the plate?

**Answer:** An artichoke

## 3. What is full of holes but still holds water?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 4. What is full of holes but holds water in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A colander

## 5. What has a core but is not an apple?

**Answer:** An apple (it is!)

## 6. What has a stem but is not a flower?

**Answer:** A wine glass

## 7. What is orange and sounds like a parrot?

**Answer:** A carrot

## 8. What gets smaller every time it takes a bath?

**Answer:** A bar of soap

## 9. What has a pit but is not a hole in the ground?

**Answer:** A peach

## 10. What can you grate but never scratch?

**Answer:** Cheese

## 11. What can you fry but never burn?

**Answer:** An egg (sunny side up)

## 12. What has ears but cannot hear in the garden?

**Answer:** Corn
