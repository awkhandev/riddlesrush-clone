## 🧸 Fun Brain Teasers for young minds (With Answers)

Think laterally, question your assumptions, and don't trust your first instinct. That's the secret to these.

## 1. What has a head and a tail but no body?

**Answer:** A coin

## 2. What has teeth but cannot bite?

**Answer:** A comb

## 3. What building has the most stories?

**Answer:** A library

## 4. What has a tongue but cannot talk?

**Answer:** A shoe

## 5. What comes down but never goes up?

**Answer:** Rain

## 6. What has a head, a tail, is brown, and has no legs?

**Answer:** A penny

## 7. What has keys but no locks?

**Answer:** A piano

## 8. What has an area but no space?

**Answer:** A room

## 9. What has a thumb and four fingers but is not alive?

**Answer:** A glove

## 10. What has wings but cannot fly?

**Answer:** A jacket

## 11. What can you hold in your right hand but never in your left?

**Answer:** Your left elbow

## 12. What can you taste but never smell?

**Answer:** A word

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Having trouble? Try these tips before giving up:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
