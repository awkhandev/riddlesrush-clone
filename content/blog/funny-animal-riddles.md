## 🐾 Funny Animal Brain Teasers (With Answers)

Think you can crack these? Give each one a shot before scrolling down to the solution.

## 1. What can run but never walks in nature?

**Answer:** A river

## 2. What has a heart but no blood in nature?

**Answer:** A tree

## 3. What is always running but never moves in nature?

**Answer:** A river

## 4. What has many rings but no fingers in the forest?

**Answer:** A tree trunk

## 5. What has a spine but no bones in the garden?

**Answer:** A cactus

## 6. What falls in a forest but never gets hurt?

**Answer:** A leaf

## 7. What has a bark but no bite?

**Answer:** A tree

## 8. What can you hear but not touch in the woods?

**Answer:** The wind

## 9. What comes down but never goes up in the sky?

**Answer:** Rain

## 10. What has roots but no legs?

**Answer:** A tree

## 11. What is full of holes but still holds water in the garden?

**Answer:** Soil

## 12. What can travel around the world while staying in a flower?

**Answer:** A seed
