## 🧠 Hard Brain Teasers for grown-ups Who Want a Brain Burn (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has one head, one foot, and four legs?

**Answer:** A bed

## 2. What word becomes shorter when you add two letters to it?

**Answer:** Short

## 3. What disappears as soon as you say its name?

**Answer:** Silence

## 4. What can you sit in but never stand in?

**Answer:** Traffic

## 5. What is it that you cannot hold for ten seconds even though it's lighter than a feather?

**Answer:** Your breath

## 6. What is orange and sounds like a parrot?

**Answer:** A carrot

## 7. What can fill your hand but not your fist?

**Answer:** Water

## 8. What gets broken without being held?

**Answer:** A promise

## 9. What has an end but no beginning?

**Answer:** A stick

## 10. What has no beginning, no end, and nothing in between?

**Answer:** A circle

## 11. What is always in front of you but can't be seen?

**Answer:** The future

## 12. What is it that you can break without even touching it?

**Answer:** A promise
