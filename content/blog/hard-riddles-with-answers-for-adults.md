## 🧠 Hard Brain Teasers with Solutions for grown-ups (With Answers)

Test your wits with these — but don't feel bad if you need to check the answers. That's what they're there for.

## 1. What can you make that you can never see?

**Answer:** A noise

## 2. What is heavy forward but backward is not?

**Answer:** Ton

## 3. What comes down but never goes up?

**Answer:** Rain

## 4. What is it that when you take away the whole, you still have some left over?

**Answer:** Some

## 5. What is black when it is clean and white when it is dirty?

**Answer:** A chalkboard

## 6. What is it that given one, you'll have either two or none?

**Answer:** A choice

## 7. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A guitar

## 8. What can run but never walks, has a mouth but never talks?

**Answer:** A river

## 9. What is it that you can keep after giving to someone?

**Answer:** Your word

## 10. What has cities, but no houses; forests, but no trees; and water, but no fish?

**Answer:** A map

## 11. What is always coming but never arrives?

**Answer:** Tomorrow

## 12. What is always running but never moves?

**Answer:** Water

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Stuck on a riddle? Here are some strategies that might help:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
