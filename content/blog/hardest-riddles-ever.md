## 🔥 20 of the (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What can you sit in but never stand in?

**Answer:** Traffic

## 2. What is it that you cannot hold for ten seconds even though it's lighter than a feather?

**Answer:** Your breath

## 3. What is orange and sounds like a parrot?

**Answer:** A carrot

## 4. What can fill your hand but not your fist?

**Answer:** Water

## 5. What gets broken without being held?

**Answer:** A promise

## 6. What has an end but no beginning?

**Answer:** A stick

## 7. What has no beginning, no end, and nothing in between?

**Answer:** A circle

## 8. What is always in front of you but can't be seen?

**Answer:** The future

## 9. What is it that you can break without even touching it?

**Answer:** A promise

## 10. What has a neck but no head, two arms but no hands?

**Answer:** A shirt

## 11. What is seen once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M

## 12. What is full of keys but can't open a single door?

**Answer:** A piano

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
