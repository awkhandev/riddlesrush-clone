## 🔥 Hardest Guess what I am! Brain Teasers (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has a heart that doesn't beat?

**Answer:** An artichoke

## 2. What is easy to lift but hard to throw?

**Answer:** A feather

## 3. What has many keys but can't open a single lock?

**Answer:** A computer keyboard

## 4. What starts with an 'e', ends with an 'e', and contains one letter?

**Answer:** An envelope

## 5. What has many rings but no fingers?

**Answer:** A tree trunk

## 6. What belongs to you but is used more by others?

**Answer:** Your name

## 7. What is light as a feather but the strongest person can't hold it for more than a minute?

**Answer:** Your breath

## 8. What has words but never speaks?

**Answer:** A book

## 9. What has one head, one foot, and four legs?

**Answer:** A bed

## 10. What word becomes shorter when you add two letters to it?

**Answer:** Short

## 11. What disappears as soon as you say its name?

**Answer:** Silence

## 12. What can you sit in but never stand in?

**Answer:** Traffic
