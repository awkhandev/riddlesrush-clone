## 🚀 Houston Riddles

Explore Houston with riddles about space, sports, and bayou views.

## 1. Space City

I'm known as Space City USA. I'm home to NASA and astronauts who fly to the moon and beyond. What city am I?

**Answer:** Houston

## 2. Mission Control

I'm where astronauts are directed from Earth. I monitor space missions and keep them on their berth. What am I?

**Answer:** Mission Control Center

## 3. The Rockets

I'm a basketball team in Houston that soars. I play at Toyota Center and have won championships before. What team am I?

**Answer:** Houston Rockets

## 4. The Space Center

I'm a museum and attraction where you can see rockets and space gear. I'm in Houston and bring visitors from far and near. What am I?

**Answer:** Space Center Houston

## 5. The Bayou City

I'm called the Bayou City for a reason. Slow-moving waterways wind through me in every season. What city am I?

**Answer:** Houston
