The classic "What Am I?" format — a series of clues describing something without naming it. This 🌴 collection is packed with brain teasers that'll have you guessing, debating, and second-guessing yourself. How many can you solve?


## 🌴 Los Angeles Riddles

Covers Hollywood, beaches, and palm-lined boulevards.

## 1. The City of Angels

I'm called the City of Angels, where dreams come alive. I'm home to Hollywood and palm trees that thrive. What city am I?

**Answer:** Los Angeles

## 2. The Hollywood Sign

I'm nine white letters on a hillside so bright. I spell out a dream for all under the night. What am I?

**Answer:** The Hollywood Sign

## 3. The Walk of Fame

I'm a sidewalk with stars embedded in brass. Celebrities leave their mark as visitors pass. What am I?

**Answer:** Hollywood Walk of Fame

## 4. The Beach City

I'm a beach in LA where surfers ride waves. I'm famous for my pier and the sun that bathes. What beach am I?

**Answer:** Santa Monica Beach

## 5. The Entertainment Capital

I'm home to movie studios where films are made. I'm the entertainment capital, unafraid. What city am I?

**Answer:** Los Angeles
