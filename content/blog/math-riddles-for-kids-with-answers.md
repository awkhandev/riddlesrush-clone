## 🔢 Math (With Answers)

Ready to put your brain to work? See how many of these you can solve without help.

## 1. What has keys but no locks?

**Answer:** A piano

## 2. What has an area but no space?

**Answer:** A room

## 3. What has a thumb and four fingers but is not alive?

**Answer:** A glove

## 4. What has wings but cannot fly?

**Answer:** A jacket

## 5. What can you hold in your right hand but never in your left?

**Answer:** Your left elbow

## 6. What can you taste but never smell?

**Answer:** A word

## 7. What has one eye but cannot see?

**Answer:** A needle

## 8. What can you break without touching it?

**Answer:** A promise

## 9. What is always in front of you but can't be seen?

**Answer:** The future

## 10. What has a bottom at the top?

**Answer:** Your legs

## 11. What has a cover but is not a book?

**Answer:** A pot

## 12. What has hands but can't clap?

**Answer:** A clock

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Having trouble? Try these tips before giving up:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
