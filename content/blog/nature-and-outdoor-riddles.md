## 1. What has a face but no eyes in the forest?

**Answer:** A cliff

## 2. What can you hear but not touch in the woods?

**Answer:** The wind

## 3. What gets wetter the more it dries in the sun?

**Answer:** A towel

## 4. What can be cracked but never broken in nature?

**Answer:** An egg

## 5.  What has a mouth but never speaks in the forest?

**Answer:** A river

## 6. What falls in a forest but never gets hurt?

**Answer:** A leaf

## 7. What has a bark but no bite?

**Answer:** A tree

## 8. What is always running but never moves in nature?

**Answer:** A river

## 9. What comes down but never goes up in the sky?

**Answer:** Rain

## 10. What has roots but no legs?

**Answer:** A tree

## 11. What is full of holes but still holds water in the garden?

**Answer:** Soil

## 12. What can travel around the world while staying in a flower?

**Answer:** A seed
