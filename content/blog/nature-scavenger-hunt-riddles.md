## 🌲 Nature Scavenger Hunt (With Answers)

Perfect for breaking out at your next gathering. Warning: these may cause heated debates about the answers.

## 1. What can you catch but not throw in nature?

**Answer:** A cold breeze

## 2. What has roots but no legs?

**Answer:** A tree

## 3. What has a face but no eyes in the garden?

**Answer:** A sunflower

## 4. What has a head and a tail but no body in the forest?

**Answer:** A caterpillar

## 5. What has an eye but cannot see in the garden?

**Answer:** A potato

## 6. What falls in a forest but never gets hurt?

**Answer:** A leaf

## 7. What has a bark but no bite?

**Answer:** A tree

## 8. What can you hear but not touch in the woods?

**Answer:** The wind

## 9. What is always running but never moves in nature?

**Answer:** A river

## 10. What comes down but never goes up in the sky?

**Answer:** Rain

## 11. What is full of holes but still holds water in the garden?

**Answer:** Soil

## 12. What can travel around the world while staying in a flower?

**Answer:** A seed

## Tips for Solving Riddles

A few techniques that seasoned riddle-solvers swear by:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
