## 🎆 New Year Math Warm (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What is a parent's favorite Christmas carol?

**Answer:** Silent Night

## 2. What do snowmen like to do on the weekend?

**Answer:** Chill out

## 3. What do you call a snowman with a six-pack?

**Answer:** An abdominal snowman

## 4. Why did the turkey join the band?

**Answer:** Because it had the drumsticks

## 5. What falls in winter but never gets hurt?

**Answer:** Snow

## 6. What do elves learn in school?

**Answer:** The elf-abet

## 7. What do you get when you cross a vampire with a snowman?

**Answer:** Frostbite

## 8. What is a Christmas tree's favorite candy?

**Answer:** O-ornaments

## 9. Why was the Christmas tree so bad at knitting?

**Answer:** Because it always dropped its needles

## 10. What do you call a dog that works at a mall?

**Answer:** A mall-y

## 11. What do ghosts serve for dessert?

**Answer:** Boo-berry pie

## 12. What do you call a reindeer with no eyes?

**Answer:** No eye-deer
