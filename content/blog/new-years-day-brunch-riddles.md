## 🎆 New Year's Day Brunch (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has cities, but no houses; forests, but no trees; and water, but no fish?

**Answer:** A map

## 2. What is always coming but never arrives?

**Answer:** Tomorrow

## 3. What is always running but never moves?

**Answer:** Water

## 4. What comes once in a year, twice in a week, but never in a day?

**Answer:** The letter E

## 5. What has a head and a tail but no body?

**Answer:** A coin

## 6. What is the end of everything?

**Answer:** The letter G

## 7. What can you never finish?

**Answer:** An autobiography

## 8. What can you hold without using your hands?

**Answer:** Your breath

## 9. What has a heart that doesn't beat?

**Answer:** An artichoke

## 10. What is easy to lift but hard to throw?

**Answer:** A feather

## 11. What has many keys but can't open a single lock?

**Answer:** A computer keyboard

## 12. What starts with an 'e', ends with an 'e', and contains one letter?

**Answer:** An envelope
