## 🗽 New York City Riddles

About boroughs, bridges, and bright lights.

## 1. The Big Apple

I'm known as the Big Apple, a city that never sleeps. I'm home to Broadway, Central Park, and skyscrapers that steep. What city am I?

**Answer:** New York City

## 2. The Lady in the Harbor

I hold a torch high in New York Harbor's embrace. I welcome immigrants to this great place. What am I?

**Answer:** Statue of Liberty

## 3. The Park in the City

I'm a green oasis in the middle of Manhattan. I'm 843 acres of nature in the urban canyon. What am I?

**Answer:** Central Park

## 4. The Bridge

I connect Manhattan and Brooklyn, a suspension feat. I'm painted orange and have been since 1883's greet. What bridge am I?

**Answer:** Brooklyn Bridge

## 5. The Bright Lights

I'm a place in Manhattan where neon lights glow. Broadway theaters shine, and the show must go on, you know. What am I?

**Answer:** Times Square
