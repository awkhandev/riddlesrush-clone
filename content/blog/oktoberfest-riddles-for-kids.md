## 🧸 Oktoberfest (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has wings but cannot fly?

**Answer:** A jacket

## 2. What can you hold in your right hand but never in your left?

**Answer:** Your left elbow

## 3. What can you taste but never smell?

**Answer:** A word

## 4. What has one eye but cannot see?

**Answer:** A needle

## 5. What can you break without touching it?

**Answer:** A promise

## 6. What is always in front of you but can't be seen?

**Answer:** The future

## 7. What has a bottom at the top?

**Answer:** Your legs

## 8. What has a cover but is not a book?

**Answer:** A pot

## 9. What has hands but can't clap?

**Answer:** A clock

## 10. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A bottle

## 11. What has a face and two hands but no arms or legs?

**Answer:** A clock

## 12. What gets smaller every time it takes a bath?

**Answer:** A bar of soap
