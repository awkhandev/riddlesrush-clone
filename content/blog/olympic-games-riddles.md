## 🧩 Olympic Games (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has a foul but no smell?

**Answer:** A sports foul

## 2. What has a slam but no door?

**Answer:** A slam dunk

## 3. What has a net but no fish?

**Answer:** A basketball hoop

## 4. What has a base but no house?

**Answer:** A baseball base

## 5. What has a tackle but no fishing?

**Answer:** A football tackle

## 6. What has a head and a tail but no body in the game?

**Answer:** A coin (toss)

## 7. What can you catch but not throw in sports?

**Answer:** A ball

## 8. What has a bat but is not a creature?

**Answer:** A baseball bat

## 9. What has a pitch but is not a tent?

**Answer:** A soccer field

## 10. What has a court but no judge?

**Answer:** A tennis court

## 11. What has a hole but no cave?

**Answer:** A golf course

## 12. What has a track but no train?

**Answer:** A running track
