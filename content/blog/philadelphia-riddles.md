The classic "What Am I?" format — a series of clues describing something without naming it. This 🔔 collection is packed with brain teasers that'll have you guessing, debating, and second-guessing yourself. How many can you solve?


## 🔔 Philadelphia Riddles

Covers liberty, murals, and food favorites.

## 1. The City of Brotherly Love

My name means "brotherly love" in Greek. I'm home to the Liberty Bell and cheesesteaks that rock. What city am I?

**Answer:** Philadelphia

## 2. The Cracked Bell

I'm a bell that cracked but still rings true. I'm a symbol of American freedom for me and you. What am I?

**Answer:** Liberty Bell

## 3. The Cheesesteak

I'm a sandwich born in Philly, with thinly sliced beef and melted cheese. Whiz or provolone, I'm a Philadelphia feast. What am I?

**Answer:** Philadelphia Cheesesteak

## 4. The Rocky Steps

I'm a set of steps at the Philadelphia Museum of Art. A fictional boxer ran up me, and I became a work of art. What am I?

**Answer:** The Rocky Steps

## 5. The Founding Document

I was signed in Independence Hall in 1776. I declared a nation free, and the world was in a fix. What am I?

**Answer:** Declaration of Independence
