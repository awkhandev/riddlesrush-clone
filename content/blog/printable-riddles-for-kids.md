Kids love a good riddle, and this collection is loaded with them. These 🧸 brain teasers are designed to get young minds thinking — tricky enough to be fun, but never so hard they give up. Grab the kids and start guessing!


## 🧸 Printable (With Answers)

Think you can crack these? Give each one a shot before scrolling down to the solution.

## 1. What has a cover but is not a book?

**Answer:** A pot

## 2. What has hands but can't clap?

**Answer:** A clock

## 3. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A bottle

## 4. What has a face and two hands but no arms or legs?

**Answer:** A clock

## 5. What gets smaller every time it takes a bath?

**Answer:** A bar of soap

## 6. What gets sharper the more you use it?

**Answer:** Your brain

## 7. What can be cracked, made, told, and played?

**Answer:** A joke

## 8. What can you catch but not throw?

**Answer:** A cold

## 9. What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M

## 10. What is full of holes but still holds water?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 11. What can fill a room but takes up no space?

**Answer:** Light

## 12. What has a ring but no finger?

**Answer:** A telephone

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Before you peek at the answers, try these approaches:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
