## 🧠 Brain Teasers for grown-ups with Solutions (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What starts with an 'e', ends with an 'e', and contains one letter?

**Answer:** An envelope

## 2. What has many rings but no fingers?

**Answer:** A tree trunk

## 3. What belongs to you but is used more by others?

**Answer:** Your name

## 4. What is light as a feather but the strongest person can't hold it for more than a minute?

**Answer:** Your breath

## 5. What has words but never speaks?

**Answer:** A book

## 6. What has one head, one foot, and four legs?

**Answer:** A bed

## 7. What word becomes shorter when you add two letters to it?

**Answer:** Short

## 8. What disappears as soon as you say its name?

**Answer:** Silence

## 9. What can you sit in but never stand in?

**Answer:** Traffic

## 10. What is it that you cannot hold for ten seconds even though it's lighter than a feather?

**Answer:** Your breath

## 11. What is orange and sounds like a parrot?

**Answer:** A carrot

## 12. What can fill your hand but not your fist?

**Answer:** Water
