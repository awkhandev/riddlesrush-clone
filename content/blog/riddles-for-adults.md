## 🧠 Brain Teasers for grown-ups With Solutions (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What is it that given one, you'll have either two or none?

**Answer:** A choice

## 2. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A guitar

## 3. What can run but never walks, has a mouth but never talks?

**Answer:** A river

## 4. What is it that you can keep after giving to someone?

**Answer:** Your word

## 5. What has cities, but no houses; forests, but no trees; and water, but no fish?

**Answer:** A map

## 6. What is always coming but never arrives?

**Answer:** Tomorrow

## 7. What is always running but never moves?

**Answer:** Water

## 8. What comes once in a year, twice in a week, but never in a day?

**Answer:** The letter E

## 9. What has a head and a tail but no body?

**Answer:** A coin

## 10. What is the end of everything?

**Answer:** The letter G

## 11. What can you never finish?

**Answer:** An autobiography

## 12. What can you hold without using your hands?

**Answer:** Your breath
