## 🌱 Brain Teasers for Garden Clubs in Full Bloom (With Answers)

Ready to put your brain to work? See how many of these you can solve without help.

## 1. What is always in front of you but can't be seen in nature?

**Answer:** The horizon

## 2. What can you hold in your hand but never see in nature?

**Answer:** Wind

## 3. What has a bark but no bite?

**Answer:** A tree

## 4. What has a head and a tail but no body in the pond?

**Answer:** A tadpole

## 5. What has keys but no locks in the wild?

**Answer:** A bird's song

## 6. What falls in a forest but never gets hurt?

**Answer:** A leaf

## 7. What can you hear but not touch in the woods?

**Answer:** The wind

## 8. What is always running but never moves in nature?

**Answer:** A river

## 9. What comes down but never goes up in the sky?

**Answer:** Rain

## 10. What has roots but no legs?

**Answer:** A tree

## 11. What is full of holes but still holds water in the garden?

**Answer:** Soil

## 12. What can travel around the world while staying in a flower?

**Answer:** A seed

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Having trouble? Try these tips before giving up:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
