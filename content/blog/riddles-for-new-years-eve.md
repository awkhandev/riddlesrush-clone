## 🎆 Brain Teasers for New Year’s Eve Countdown Fun (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What do you call a candy cane that tells jokes?

**Answer:** A laughingstock

## 2. What do you call a dog that works at a mall?

**Answer:** A mall-y

## 3. Why do mummies love vacations so much?

**Answer:** Because they like to unwind

## 4. Why does Scrooge love Rudolph?

**Answer:** Because he is red and green and smells like venison

## 5. What do you call a bunch of chess players bragging about their wins?

**Answer:** Chess nuts boasting in an open foyer

## 6. What do you call a snowman with a six-pack?

**Answer:** An abdominal snowman

## 7. What do elves learn in school?

**Answer:** The elf-abet

## 8. What do you get when you cross a vampire with a snowman?

**Answer:** Frostbite

## 9. What is a Christmas tree's favorite candy?

**Answer:** O-ornaments

## 10. Why was the Christmas tree so bad at knitting?

**Answer:** Because it always dropped its needles

## 11. What do ghosts serve for dessert?

**Answer:** Boo-berry pie

## 12. Why did the turkey join the band?

**Answer:** Because it had the drumsticks
