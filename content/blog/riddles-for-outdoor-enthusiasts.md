## ❤️ Brain Teasers for Outdoor Enthusiasts and Trail Lovers (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What can fill a room but takes up no space outdoors?

**Answer:** Sunlight

## 2. What gets smaller every time it rains?

**Answer:** A mud puddle

## 3. What has a bed but never sleeps outdoors?

**Answer:** A riverbed

## 4. What has leaves but no branches?

**Answer:** A book in the forest

## 5. What comes down but never goes up in the sky?

**Answer:** Rain

## 6. What falls in a forest but never gets hurt?

**Answer:** A leaf

## 7. What has a bark but no bite?

**Answer:** A tree

## 8. What can you hear but not touch in the woods?

**Answer:** The wind

## 9. What is always running but never moves in nature?

**Answer:** A river

## 10. What has roots but no legs?

**Answer:** A tree

## 11. What is full of holes but still holds water in the garden?

**Answer:** Soil

## 12. What can travel around the world while staying in a flower?

**Answer:** A seed
