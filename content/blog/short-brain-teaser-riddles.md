## 🧠 Short Brain Teaser (With Answers)

Some of these will trick you, some will make you groan, and some will genuinely surprise you. Let's go!

## 1. What is black when it is clean and white when it is dirty?

**Answer:** A chalkboard

## 2. What is it that given one, you'll have either two or none?

**Answer:** A choice

## 3. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A guitar

## 4. What can run but never walks, has a mouth but never talks?

**Answer:** A river

## 5. What is it that you can keep after giving to someone?

**Answer:** Your word

## 6. What has cities, but no houses; forests, but no trees; and water, but no fish?

**Answer:** A map

## 7. What is always coming but never arrives?

**Answer:** Tomorrow

## 8. What is always running but never moves?

**Answer:** Water

## 9. What comes once in a year, twice in a week, but never in a day?

**Answer:** The letter E

## 10. What has a head and a tail but no body?

**Answer:** A coin

## 11. What is the end of everything?

**Answer:** The letter G

## 12. What can you never finish?

**Answer:** An autobiography

## Tips for Solving Riddles

A few techniques that seasoned riddle-solvers swear by:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
