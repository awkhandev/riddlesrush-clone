Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What is easy to get into but hard to get out of?

**Answer:** Trouble

## 2. What has many teeth but cannot bite?

**Answer:** A zipper

## 3. What can travel around the world while staying in a corner?

**Answer:** A stamp

## 4. What has ears but cannot hear?

**Answer:** Corn

## 5. What has a spine but no bones?

**Answer:** A book

## 6. What can you hear but not touch or see?

**Answer:** Your voice

## 7. What goes up but never comes down?

**Answer:** Your age

## 8. What has a head and a tail but no body?

**Answer:** A coin

## 9. What has teeth but cannot bite?

**Answer:** A comb

## 10. What building has the most stories?

**Answer:** A library

## 11. What has a tongue but cannot talk?

**Answer:** A shoe

## 12. What comes down but never goes up?

**Answer:** Rain

## Tips for Solving Riddles

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
