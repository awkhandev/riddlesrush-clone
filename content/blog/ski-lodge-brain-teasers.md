## 🧠 Ski Lodge (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What is orange and sounds like a parrot?

**Answer:** A carrot

## 2. What can fill your hand but not your fist?

**Answer:** Water

## 3. What gets broken without being held?

**Answer:** A promise

## 4. What has an end but no beginning?

**Answer:** A stick

## 5. What has no beginning, no end, and nothing in between?

**Answer:** A circle

## 6. What is always in front of you but can't be seen?

**Answer:** The future

## 7. What is it that you can break without even touching it?

**Answer:** A promise

## 8. What has a neck but no head, two arms but no hands?

**Answer:** A shirt

## 9. What is seen once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M

## 10. What is full of keys but can't open a single door?

**Answer:** A piano

## 11. What can you make that you can never see?

**Answer:** A noise

## 12. What is heavy forward but backward is not?

**Answer:** Ton
