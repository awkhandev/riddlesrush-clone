## ❄️ Snowy Playground (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. I can travel around the world while staying in a corner. What am I?

**Answer:** A stamp

## 2. I have keys but I open no doors. What am I?

**Answer:** A piano

## 3. I have teeth but I cannot bite. What am I?

**Answer:** A comb

## 4. I have a head and a tail but no body. What am I?

**Answer:** A coin

## 5. I am full of holes but I can still hold water. What am I?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 6. I have cities, but no houses. I have mountains, but no trees. I have water, but no fish. What am I?

**Answer:** A map

## 7. The more you take, the more you leave behind. What am I?

**Answer:** Footsteps

## 8. I fly all day but I never go anywhere. What am I?

**Answer:** A flag

## 9. I can be cracked, I can be made. I can be told, I can be played. What am I?

**Answer:** A joke

## 10. I have keys but no locks. I have space but no room. You can enter but can't go outside. What am I?

**Answer:** A keyboard

## 11. I am always running but never move. What am I?

**Answer:** Water

## 12. I have hands but I can't clap. What am I?

**Answer:** A clock
