## ☀️ Summer Riddles

Fun summer riddles about sunshine, beaches, ice cream, and warm-weather adventures.

## 1. The Hot Sphere

I shine bright in the sky, warming beaches day by day. Without me, there'd be no summer fun or outdoor play. What am I?

**Answer:** The Sun

## 2. Cold Treat

I'm cold and sweet on a stick, I come in flavors galore. On a hot summer day, you'll find me at the store. What am I?

**Answer:** Popsicle / Ice Cream

## 3. Sandy Place

I'm found at the beach, where waves crash and roll. You build castles on me, and I'm warm to the soul. What am I?

**Answer:** Sand

## 4. Pool Float

I keep you floating on the water, I'm colorful and light. I'm made of air and plastic, and I make swimming feel right. What am I?

**Answer:** Pool Float / Inflatable Raft

## 5. The Buzzing Sound

I buzz around in summer, wearing yellow and black. I make honey in my hive, and I've got a tiny attack. What am I?

**Answer:** Bee

## 6. Summer Fashion

I protect your eyes from the sun, I sit on your nose with ease. On a summer day, you'll see me on faces with breeze. What am I?

**Answer:** Sunglasses

## 7. The Ice Cream Truck

I play a jingle down the street, bringing cold treats in the heat. Kids chase me with coins in hand, running barefoot on the street. What am I?

**Answer:** Ice Cream Truck

## 8. The Longest Day

I'm the day with the most sunlight, falling in late June. After me, the days get shorter — summer starts to swoon. What day am I?

**Answer:** Summer Solstice
