Hungry for a challenge? These ☀️ food riddles serve up brain teasers about everything from fruits and vegetables to kitchen gadgets and recipes. Great for dinner parties, cooking classes, or anytime you want to tickle your taste buds and your brain.


## ☀️ Sunday Brunch (With Answers)

Grab a friend and take turns guessing. The real fun is in the debate over the answers.

## 1. What is full of holes but still holds water?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 2. What is full of holes but holds water in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A colander

## 3. What has a core but is not an apple?

**Answer:** An apple (it is!)

## 4. What has a stem but is not a flower?

**Answer:** A wine glass

## 5. What is orange and sounds like a parrot?

**Answer:** A carrot

## 6. What gets smaller every time it takes a bath?

**Answer:** A bar of soap

## 7. What has a pit but is not a hole in the ground?

**Answer:** A peach

## 8. What can you grate but never scratch?

**Answer:** Cheese

## 9. What can you fry but never burn?

**Answer:** An egg (sunny side up)

## 10. What has ears but cannot hear in the garden?

**Answer:** Corn

## 11. What has a tongue but cannot talk at the table?

**Answer:** A shoe (slipper)

## 12. What can you spread but not throw on bread?

**Answer:** Butter

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Before you peek at the answers, try these approaches:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
