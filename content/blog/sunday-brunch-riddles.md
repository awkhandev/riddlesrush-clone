## ☀️ Sunday Brunch (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What is full of holes but still holds water?

**Answer:** A sponge

## 2. What is full of holes but holds water in the kitchen?

**Answer:** A colander

## 3. What has a core but is not an apple?

**Answer:** An apple (it is!)

## 4. What has a stem but is not a flower?

**Answer:** A wine glass

## 5. What is orange and sounds like a parrot?

**Answer:** A carrot

## 6. What gets smaller every time it takes a bath?

**Answer:** A bar of soap

## 7. What has a pit but is not a hole in the ground?

**Answer:** A peach

## 8. What can you grate but never scratch?

**Answer:** Cheese

## 9. What can you fry but never burn?

**Answer:** An egg (sunny side up)

## 10. What has ears but cannot hear in the garden?

**Answer:** Corn

## 11. What has a tongue but cannot talk at the table?

**Answer:** A shoe (slipper)

## 12. What can you spread but not throw on bread?

**Answer:** Butter
