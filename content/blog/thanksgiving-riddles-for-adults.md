## 🦃 Thanksgiving (With Answers)

Test your knowledge with these fun riddles! Each one is designed to make you think and smile.

## 1. What has many keys but can't open a single lock?

**Answer:** A computer keyboard

## 2. What starts with an 'e', ends with an 'e', and contains one letter?

**Answer:** An envelope

## 3. What has many rings but no fingers?

**Answer:** A tree trunk

## 4. What belongs to you but is used more by others?

**Answer:** Your name

## 5. What is light as a feather but the strongest person can't hold it for more than a minute?

**Answer:** Your breath

## 6. What has words but never speaks?

**Answer:** A book

## 7. What has one head, one foot, and four legs?

**Answer:** A bed

## 8. What word becomes shorter when you add two letters to it?

**Answer:** Short

## 9. What disappears as soon as you say its name?

**Answer:** Silence

## 10. What can you sit in but never stand in?

**Answer:** Traffic

## 11. What is it that you cannot hold for ten seconds even though it's lighter than a feather?

**Answer:** Your breath

## 12. What is orange and sounds like a parrot?

**Answer:** A carrot

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
