## 📱 TikTok (With Answers)

Grab a friend and take turns guessing. The real fun is in the debate over the answers.

## 1. What has a neck but no head, two arms but no hands?

**Answer:** A shirt

## 2. What is seen once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M

## 3. What is full of keys but can't open a single door?

**Answer:** A piano

## 4. What can you make that you can never see?

**Answer:** A noise

## 5. What is heavy forward but backward is not?

**Answer:** Ton

## 6. What comes down but never goes up?

**Answer:** Rain

## 7. What is it that when you take away the whole, you still have some left over?

**Answer:** Some

## 8. What is black when it is clean and white when it is dirty?

**Answer:** A chalkboard

## 9. What is it that given one, you'll have either two or none?

**Answer:** A choice

## 10. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A guitar

## 11. What can run but never walks, has a mouth but never talks?

**Answer:** A river

## 12. What is it that you can keep after giving to someone?

**Answer:** Your word

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Before you peek at the answers, try these approaches:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
