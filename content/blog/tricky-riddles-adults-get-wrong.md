## 🧠 Tricky Brain Teasers That Adults Always Get Wrong (With Answers)

Ready to put your brain to work? See how many of these you can solve without help.

## 1. What can fill your hand but not your fist?

**Answer:** Water

## 2. What gets broken without being held?

**Answer:** A promise

## 3. What has an end but no beginning?

**Answer:** A stick

## 4. What has no beginning, no end, and nothing in between?

**Answer:** A circle

## 5. What is always in front of you but can't be seen?

**Answer:** The future

## 6. What is it that you can break without even touching it?

**Answer:** A promise

## 7. What has a neck but no head, two arms but no hands?

**Answer:** A shirt

## 8. What is seen once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M

## 9. What is full of keys but can't open a single door?

**Answer:** A piano

## 10. What can you make that you can never see?

**Answer:** A noise

## 11. What is heavy forward but backward is not?

**Answer:** Ton

## 12. What comes down but never goes up?

**Answer:** Rain

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Having trouble? Try these tips before giving up:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
