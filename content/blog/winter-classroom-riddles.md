Kids love a good riddle, and this collection is loaded with them. These ❄️ brain teasers are designed to get young minds thinking — tricky enough to be fun, but never so hard they give up. Grab the kids and start guessing!


## ❄️ Winter Classroom (With Answers)

Think you can crack these? Give each one a shot before scrolling down to the solution.

## 1. What has a face like a clock, hands like a clock, but tells time differently?

**Answer:** A calendar

## 2. What gets wetter the more it dries?

**Answer:** A towel

## 3. What begins with T, ends with T, and is full of T?

**Answer:** A teapot

## 4. What can you keep after giving to someone?

**Answer:** Your word

## 5. What is seen in the middle of March and April but not at the beginning or end of either month?

**Answer:** The letter R

## 6. What is easy to get into but hard to get out of?

**Answer:** Trouble

## 7. What has many teeth but cannot bite?

**Answer:** A zipper

## 8. What can travel around the world while staying in a corner?

**Answer:** A stamp

## 9. What has ears but cannot hear?

**Answer:** Corn

## 10. What has a spine but no bones?

**Answer:** A book

## 11. What can you hear but not touch or see?

**Answer:** Your voice

## 12. What goes up but never comes down?

**Answer:** Your age

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Before you peek at the answers, try these approaches:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
