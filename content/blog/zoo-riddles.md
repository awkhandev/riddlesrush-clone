## ❤️ Zoo (With Answers)

Here are some brain teasers that'll keep you guessing. Try to solve each one before peeking at the answer!

## 1. What can you break without touching it?

**Answer:** A promise

## 2. What is always in front of you but can't be seen?

**Answer:** The future

## 3. What has a bottom at the top?

**Answer:** Your legs

## 4. What has a cover but is not a book?

**Answer:** A pot

## 5. What has hands but can't clap?

**Answer:** A clock

## 6. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A bottle

## 7. What has a face and two hands but no arms or legs?

**Answer:** A clock

## 8. What gets smaller every time it takes a bath?

**Answer:** A bar of soap

## 9. What gets sharper the more you use it?

**Answer:** Your brain

## 10. What can be cracked, made, told, and played?

**Answer:** A joke

## 11. What can you catch but not throw?

**Answer:** A cold

## 12. What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M

## Tips for Solving Riddles

Stuck on a riddle? Here are some strategies that might help:

1. **Read carefully** - Pay attention to every word in the riddle
2. **Think laterally** - Sometimes the answer isn't what you expect
3. **Consider multiple meanings** - Words can have more than one definition
4. **Don't overthink it** - Sometimes the simplest answer is correct
5. **Have fun** - The goal is to enjoy the process, not just find the answer
