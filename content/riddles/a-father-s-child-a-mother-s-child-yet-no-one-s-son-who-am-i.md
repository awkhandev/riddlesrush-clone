A father's child, a mother's child, yet no one's son. Who am I?

Take a moment to think about this classic riddle. The answer might be simpler than you think!

**Answer:** A daughter.

## Why This Riddle Works

This riddle plays on our assumptions about family relationships. We hear "father's child" and "mother's child" and assume the answer must be a son, but the riddle specifically says "no one's son." The answer is a daughter — she is both her father's child and her mother's child, but she is not anyone's son.

## Tips for Solving Riddles Like This

1. **Read every word carefully** — Riddles often use specific wording to misdirect you
2. **Think beyond the literal meaning** — The answer is usually a play on words
3. **Challenge your assumptions** — Don't assume gender or other attributes
4. **Don't overthink it** — Sometimes the simplest answer is the right one
5. **Have fun** — The joy is in the puzzle, not just the answer
