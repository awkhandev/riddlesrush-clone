A hundred feet in the air, but its back is on the ground. What is it?

Take a moment to think about this classic riddle. The answer might be simpler than you think!

**Answer:** A centipede flipped over.

## Why This Riddle Works

This riddle plays on the word "feet" and our mental image of something being high in the air. A centipede literally has a hundred feet, and when it's flipped over, its feet are in the air while its back is on the ground. The trick is to think literally rather than figuratively.

## Tips for Solving Riddles Like This

1. **Read every word carefully** — Riddles often use specific wording to misdirect you
