A woman shoots her husband, then she holds him underwater for over 5 minutes, and finally she hangs him. But 5 minutes later, they go out and enjoy a wonderful dinner together. How can this be?

Take a moment to think about this classic riddle. The answer might be simpler than you think!

**Answer:** She took a photo of him — shot with a camera, developed it in water, and hung it to dry.
