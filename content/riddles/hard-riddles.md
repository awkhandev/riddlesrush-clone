## 1. The Barrel Riddle

A barrel of water weighs 20 pounds. What must you add to it to make it weigh 12 pounds?

**Answer:** A hole — the water drains out, making it weigh less.

## 2. The Man in the Photo

Look at this photo. The man's father is my father's son. Who is the man?

**Answer:** My son

## 3. The Cow's Name

A farmer has three cows. The first is named Monday, the second is named Tuesday, and the third is named Wednesday. What is the fourth cow's name?

**Answer:** The farmer only has three cows.

## 4. The Color of the Horse

A man is riding a horse. The horse is white. What color is the horse?

**Answer:** White

## 5. The Egg in the Bottle

How can you get a hard-boiled egg into a bottle without breaking the egg or the bottle?

**Answer:** Light a piece of paper, drop it in the bottle, and place the egg on top. The fire will go out, creating a vacuum that pulls the egg inside.

## 6. The River Crossing

A farmer needs to cross a river with a fox, a chicken, and a bag of grain. The boat can only carry one item at a time. If left alone, the fox will eat the chicken, and the chicken will eat the grain. How does he get everything across?

**Answer:** Take the chicken first. Come back, take the fox. Bring the chicken back. Take the grain. Come back, take the chicken.

## 7. The Three Doors

You're on a game show. There are three doors. Behind one is a car, behind the others are goats. You pick door 1. The host, who knows what's behind the doors, opens door 3, which has a goat. He says, "Do you want to stick with door 1 or switch to door 2?" Should you switch?

**Answer:** Yes, switch. You have a 2/3 chance of winning by switching versus 1/3 by staying.

## 8. The Birthday Paradox

How many people do you need in a room for there to be a 50% chance that two share a birthday?

**Answer:** Only 23 people
