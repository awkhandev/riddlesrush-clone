## 1. What Has Hands?

What has hands but can't clap?

**Answer:** A clock

## 2. What Has a Neck?

What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A bottle

## 3. The Egg

What can you break without touching it?

**Answer:** An egg

## 4. The Silent Letter

What letter is always silent in the word "environment"?

**Answer:** The letter N

## 5. The Flying Coin

What flies without wings?

**Answer:** Time

## 6. The Talking Color

What color can you taste?

**Answer:** Purple (like grape flavor)

## 7. The Tallest Short Thing

What is tall when young and short when old?

**Answer:** A candle

## 8. The Funny Face

What has an eye but cannot see?

**Answer:** A needle
