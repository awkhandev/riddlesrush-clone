Logic riddles aren't about clever wordplay — they're about following a chain of reasoning to its conclusion. Each one gives you a set of facts and asks you to deduce something that isn't immediately obvious. If you enjoy thinking through problems systematically, this is your collection.

## 1. The Elevator Riddle

A man lives on the 10th floor. Every day he takes the elevator down to the lobby to go to work. When he comes home, he takes the elevator to the 7th floor and walks up the stairs the rest of the way. Why?

**Answer:** He's too short to reach the button for the 10th floor.

## 2. The Farmer's Dilemma

A farmer has 17 sheep. All but 9 run away. How many sheep does the farmer have left?

**Answer:** 9 sheep

## 3. The Two Coins

If you have two coins that add up to 30 cents, and one of the coins is not a nickel, what are the two coins?

**Answer:** A quarter and a nickel. (Only one of them is not a nickel.)

## 4. The Missing Dollar

Three friends check into a hotel room that costs $30. They each pay $10. The manager realizes the room only costs $25 and gives $5 to the bellboy to return. The bellboy keeps $2 and gives $1 back to each friend. So each friend paid $9 (total $27) and the bellboy has $2. That's $29. Where's the missing dollar?

**Answer:** There is no missing dollar. The $27 the friends paid includes the $25 for the room and the $2 the bellboy kept. Adding $27 + $2 double-counts the bellboy's money.

## 5. The Light Switch

You're in a room with three light switches. Each controls one of three light bulbs in the next room. You can't see into the next room. How do you figure out which switch controls which bulb? You can only enter the next room once.

**Answer:** Turn on switch 1 and switch 2. Wait 10 minutes. Turn off switch 2 and enter the room. The bulb that's on is controlled by switch 1. The bulb that's off but warm is controlled by switch 2. The bulb that's off and cold is controlled by switch 3.

## 6. The Clock Hands

How many times do the hands of a clock overlap in a 12-hour period?

**Answer:** 11 times
