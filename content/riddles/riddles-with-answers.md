## 1. What has hands but can't clap?

**Answer:** A clock

## 2. What has a head and a tail but no body?

**Answer:** A coin

## 3. What can you catch but not throw?

**Answer:** A cold

## 4. What has keys but no locks?

**Answer:** A piano

## 5. What gets wetter the more it dries?

**Answer:** A towel

## 6. What has one eye but cannot see?

**Answer:** A needle

## 7. What can travel around the world while staying in a corner?

**Answer:** A stamp

## 8. What has a neck but no head?

**Answer:** A bottle

## 9. What has teeth but cannot bite?

**Answer:** A comb

## 10. What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

**Answer:** The letter M
