Sometimes you don't need a ten-paragraph puzzle. Sometimes you just need a quick riddle that lands in two seconds and makes everyone laugh. That's exactly what this collection delivers — short, punchy brain teasers that work as conversation starters, icebreakers, or just a quick mental workout between tasks.

## 1. Keys but No Locks

What has keys but no locks?

**Answer:** A keyboard

## 2. Traveling Corner

What can travel around the world while staying in a corner?

**Answer:** A stamp

## 3. Getting Wetter

What gets wetter the more it dries?

**Answer:** A towel

## 4. Echo

I speak without a mouth and hear without ears. What am I?

**Answer:** An echo

## 5. The More You Take

The more you take, the more you leave behind. What am I?

**Answer:** Footsteps

## 6. Heads but No Tail

What has a head and a tail but no body?

**Answer:** A coin

## 7. Running but Never Moving

I'm always running but I never move. What am I?

**Answer:** A clock

## 8. Blue and Green

What can be blue, green, or brown but is never red?

**Answer:** A river
