“What Am I?” riddles are the gateway drug of puzzle-solving. One person reads a series of clues, and everyone else tries to guess the answer before the reveal. The beauty is in the simplicity: no special knowledge, no equipment, just your brain and a willingness to think differently.

## I get smaller every time I take a bath. What am I?

**Answer:** A bar of soap
